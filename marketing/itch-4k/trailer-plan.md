# PokeVerse gameplay trailer: plan

itch.io embeds a YouTube or Vimeo link, so the trailer goes up on YouTube and the link goes in the page's "Gameplay video or trailer" field.

## Format
- 60 to 75 seconds; most viewers leave after the first 30, so the best moment comes first.
- 3840x2160 at 30 fps, recorded lossless from the same 4K client used for the screenshots. Exported H.264 at about 45 Mbit/s, with a 1080p copy for Discord and socials.
- The same staging as the stills: Red in the all-red outfit, a clean chat, and no staff commands on screen.
- The website, **pokeverse.quest**, is on screen twice: small under the logo on the title card, and large on the end card. It also goes on the first line of the YouTube description.
- Music: royalty-free or our own track, not Pokemon soundtrack audio, so YouTube doesn't claim it. In-game sound effects (Mega burst, thunder, cries) mixed under it.

## Structure (about 70 s)
| Time | Shot | Feature |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-6 s | Close on Red, "Charizard, Mega Evolve!", rainbow burst, Mega Charizard X lands | Mega Evolution (the hook) |
| 6-12 s | Mega Charizard X's blue fire against a wild Venusaur, damage numbers and a crit | Real-time battles |
| 12-14 s | Title card: PokeVerse logo, "A Pokemon MMO", pokeverse.quest underneath | |
| 14-22 s | The "Zapdos Wild Legendary!" card pops up, then the lightning wall on Route 1 | Wild legendaries |
| 22-28 s | Rain rolls in over the route; cut to the night clock and back to day | Live weather and day/night |
| 28-34 s | Walking into the dark cave with Gyarados, the light circle following | Caves |
| 34-40 s | The Halloween Union Room, pan across the decorations | Seasonal events |
| 40-50 s | A quick UI montage of about 2 s each: Pokepedia map, quest log, Trainer Card, vocation window, Mega gauge filling | Systems |
| 50-58 s | Catching a wild Pokemon: throw, shake, caught | Catching |
| 58-64 s | Several trainers together in a town (needs a few staff alts or a playtest crowd) | MMO |
| 64-70 s | End card: logo, "Free to play", **pokeverse.quest** as the main link, with the itch.io and Discord links smaller under it | Call to action |

## How it would be made
1. Add each shot as a scene to the same script that drove the screenshots, using the start/stop recording markers that already captured the 8-second Mega burst losslessly.
2. Record each shot 2 or 3 times, then pick the best take.
3. Cut, crossfade and add the title cards with ffmpeg (or hand the clips to an editor). Mix in the music and a sound-effect track.
4. Open questions for you: the music, whether the town crowd shot is worth staging, and whether to include a short voice-over or text-only captions.
