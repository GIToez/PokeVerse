Every file is under 2,850,000 bytes, under 2.9 MB however the size is counted. Colour is kept in full:
- The quest log and Trainer Card shots are lossless PNG.
- The rest are JPEG at quality 95 to 99 with full-resolution colour (4:4:4), and no palette reduction.
