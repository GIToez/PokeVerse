# Design Asset Audit (PSD/PSB vs. client)

The 36 original Photoshop design sources (35 PSD/PSB files and 1 PNG, 2,626,168,488 bytes in total) are **not in the working tree**. They exist in Git LFS history at commit `5e2eaee` under `assets/design-psd/`, and in the original archive (pristine extraction `_import/extracted/PSDS/PSDS/`, listed in `original/MANIFEST.sha256.tsv` under `PSDS/PSDS/`). To restore them, run `git checkout 5e2eaee -- assets/design-psd && git lfs pull --include="assets/design-psd/**"`. `.lfsconfig` sets `fetchexclude = assets/design-psd/**`, so if the pull skips them, add `--exclude=""`.

Status meanings:
- **Implemented**: the design's screens have a matching client module whose sliced images or OTUI match the design. In most rows a module background is exactly the same pixel size as the PSD canvas or one of its smart objects.
- **Partial**: the feature exists, but the design's skin or some of its screens or sections were not built.
- **Unused**: no client UI corresponds to the design.

Module paths are relative to `client/runtime-data/modules/`, and `/images/...` is relative to `client/runtime-data/data/`.

## Designed but not implemented

These features or screens appear in the PSDs, but the client has no OTUI, images or logic for them:

1. **Change Addons window** (`ADDONS.psd`). A window that applies Pokémon movement addons, with slot grid, a four-step flow, lock icons and Fly / Surf / Ride test previews. The client only has a bottom-menu button that sends `/addon`.
2. **Pokéballs Used / Capture Information** (`POKEBOLAS.psd`). A per-species capture log (Name, No., Pokéballs, Progress, Older/Newer paging) and a capture-info card (experience, price, pokéballs used, habitat art). The bottom-menu `brokes_info` button is a placeholder with the tooltip "Capture information: not active".
3. **Pokébag / Poké Center slot window** (`PokeCenter - POKE SLOT.psd`). Dedicated Pokébola, Revive, Fishing rod and Bike slots plus an addon/star grid. The client has no such slots.
4. **Starter selection screen** (`POKÉMON SELECT.psb`). A full-screen three-pokéball starter picker. The client only uses a dropdown in `poke_create`.
5. **Options tabs "Character", "Interface" and "Drivers"** (`CONFIGURAÇÕES.psd`), and the skinned options window. `client_options` has Game/Console/Graphics/Audio only.
6. **Skinned Hotkeys and NPC Trade windows** (`HOTKEYS.psd`, `TRADE NPC.psd`). The custom-art windows (select-item pointer, target icon, row-based trade list with Item / Name / Unit Price) were never sliced. The client keeps the stock OTUI.
7. **Shop "DOAR" (donate) button and the "Ironhard" clan** (`LOJA.psd`). No donate button or handler exists in `game_shop`. The `IRONHARD_SELECT` mockup has no counterpart in `game_shop/images/clas/icons` (avalanche, blaze, gaia, heremit, hurricane, spectrum, vital, voltagic, zen).
8. **"Missão concluída!" toast** (`death window/DEATH WINDOW.psb`). The mission-complete popup with the `task_kill` icon is not shown as designed. Separately, no custom death window exists; `game_playerdeath` is stock.
9. **HUD pieces in `NEW INTERFACE.psd` with no matching asset**: the "POKE LIFE" widget, the "level up" card (175×219; `game_advanceeffect` uses its own OTUI), the vertical PokeBar variant (157×354), and the unnamed panels `Group 1` (533×117), `Group 2` (259×267) and `2` (195×233).
10. **Shiny portrait set and use of `ICONdex`** (`Portraits1/PORTRAITS.psd`). The "1 GERAÇÃO - SHINY" group is empty, and the exported Gen 1–3 `ICONdex` icons are not referenced by any module.
11. **Pokédex "unknown" question-mark icon** (`POKEDEX-ICON.psd`). It is hidden in the PSD and not used; unknown entries use item 16410.

## Notes

- **Method.** Sizes and SHA-256 come from `original/MANIFEST.sha256.tsv`. Two files (`dollcase.psd`, `BOTTOMMENU_DIAMOND.psd`) were re-hashed from `_import/extracted/PSDS/PSDS/` and match. Layer names were read with `psd-tools` 1.24.0 (installed with `pip --target` into `/tmp`, outside the repo), with no compositing or rendering. Top-level groups and second-level names were read for every file. Up to three levels, text-layer strings and smart-object names/bounding boxes were read for game-specific designs. Mappings were confirmed by matching PSD canvas or smart-object pixel sizes to module PNG sizes (`file`), and by grepping Lua/OTUI for distinctive layer names.
- **Parse failures.** None in the end. `kit design/UI Elements Dark PRINCIPAL.psd` first failed with `ParseLimitError: max_objects exceeded` (more than 1,000,000 descriptor objects). It parsed with `PSDImage.open(path, parse_limits=ParseLimits(max_objects=None))`. `ENTERGAME.png` is a flat image, so there are no layers to inspect.
- **Mockup-style files.** `ACCOUNT AND CHARACTER`, `DUNGEONS`, `NEW INTERFACE`, `CASA`, `DEPOT LOCK` and parts of `MARKET`/`LOJA` are made of smart objects (`*.psb` embeds) at the top level, so their content was identified from smart-object names and pixel sizes rather than from deeper layers.
- **Mapping corrections from the initial guesses.** `DAILY KILL` maps to `game_task` (identical 991×639 canvas and 607×146 cards), not `game_pokekill`. `PERSONAGEM RELOAD` is the `client_background` reloading screen. `BLOCK DROP` is the drop-confirmation dialogs in `game_interface`. `MOVE ITEM AGRUPAVEL` is the stack/count dialog. `DEATH WINDOW.psb` actually contains a mission-complete toast.
- Statuses are design-vs-client only. Server-side correctness is covered in [UI_AUDIT.md](UI_AUDIT.md) and [FEATURE_AUDIT.md](FEATURE_AUDIT.md).
