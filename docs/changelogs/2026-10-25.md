# October 25, 2026

Covers `main` after the October 23 changelog, up to `2c207bea` (Oct 25, 11:57 AM PT). Draft: post
it once the client release and the server deploy are out. Three posts.

## Post 1 of 3

```text
📋 **PokeVerse Changelog**
**October 25, 2026**

⚔️ **New Battle Text**
- Trainers call out their moves in speech bubbles, with the move name in its type's color.
- Calling out, recalling and ordering your Pokémon, and throwing a Poké Ball, show as bubbles too. NPC trainers and Gym Leaders use them as well.
- New damage numbers: super effective hits are bigger and gold, resisted hits are smaller and grey, and critical hits are the biggest, with a small screen shake.
- Healing shows a green number, and immune hits say NO EFFECT.
- New tags for MISS, DODGED, PROTECTED, statuses like PARALYZED and ASLEEP, and stat changes like ATTACK UP.
- NPCs talking out loud use speech bubbles too.
- Prefer the old look? Options → Interface → Battle text: Classic. The screen shake can be turned off there as well.

📰 **News Banner**
- Server announcements now open from a Poké Ball at the top of the screen, instead of red text in the middle.
- Click a banner to read the whole message in the Server Log.
- Raids and tournaments get banners, and raid messages now say what is happening.
- The World Boss banner hints where to look for it.
- Tips show at most once an hour, and never during a fight.
- Don't want it? Options → Interface → Show news banner.
```

## Post 2 of 3

```text
🎃 **Halloween**
- The pirate quest can be finished now! Each faction's sailor waits at its beach and takes you aboard once you've joined that faction with Peter Paul.
- All four ships have their crews, including a new Cold Waters crew east of Cerulean.

- The Union Room is decorated for Halloween, with Trevor and Peter Paul inside.
- Every holiday token NPC opens the new Event Token Shop: item icons, prices, your token balance and a search.
- Ghost raids now appear in the Kanto towns, and one raid no longer blocks the others.
- The rare candy limit resets every Halloween.
- Holiday NPCs, like Timothy Kelvin and Murray Aaron, only appear while their event is on.

🌟 **Legendaries**
- Trainers nearby now see a legendary's signs even when it is out of sight, pointing the way to it.
- Legendaries leave a trail of signs where they have traveled.
- Sightings are only announced when a trainer saw it, or when it was close to a town.

📊 **New: Analyser**
- The new Analyser button in the top bar shows your hunt since login: trainer and Pokémon EXP, the time to your next level, and your catch chances on the last Pokémon you defeated, ball by ball.
```

## Post 3 of 3

```text
📖 **Pokédex & PC**
- Pokémon you've caught show their face in the Pokédex.
- Your starter and the Pokémon you evolve count as caught. Existing characters get credit at their next login.
- Fixed Pokédex entries that showed another Pokémon's status.
- All 40 PC boxes are open from the start.

🧭 **Interface & Controls**
- A green cross shows next to a Pokémon or trainer a potion is healing.
- The buff bar sits under your trainer HUD. Drag it anywhere, right-click to reset.
- Rangers: the Overlay button keeps a see-through radar disc on the map.
- WASD mode: Shift+WASD or Shift+arrows turn your Pokémon.
- The numpad walk keys can be changed or cleared in Settings → Controls.
- Pokémon play their cry when they shout their name.
- New map mark icons: Pokémon Center, Mart, gym badges and more.
- The minimap saves what you've explored every few minutes, so a crash won't reset it.
- Area names now show across Hoenn and the seas.

🎨 **Art & Places**
- Charmeleon has a new, bigger sprite, and Deoxys has new art.
- Officer Jenny shows her portrait.
- The ball pillar is now the Pokémon Habitat: place it in your house and drop a charged ball on it. That Pokémon walks your house, and its real ball shows on the stand.
- Fixed the dark rock patches on Viridian Forest paths.

🔧 **Changes & Fixes**
- Weekend events run Friday 5 PM to Monday 5 AM (server time).
- Area moves like Earthquake no longer hit wild Pokémon during duels and NPC battles, and wild Pokémon can't join those fights.
- Fixed a Windows crash during large updates.
- Android: a new version no longer downloads all the map pictures again, and links open in your browser.
- Big legendaries are no longer cut off in windows and cards.

The update downloads on its own the next time you start the game.
```
