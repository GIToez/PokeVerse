# October 30, 2026

Covers `main` after the October 28 changelog, up to `e18c32e7` (Oct 30, 8:00 PM PT): Gen 4, six new Megas,
legendary moves, a big art refresh, the Surfboard, phones and fixes. Gen 4 has no wild spawns yet, so it's
announced through evolutions. The Christmas Pokémon wait for the Christmas event, and the Slowpoke and Ursaring
onesies are only given by staff for now, so they're left out. Three posts.

## Post 1 of 3

```text
📋 **PokeVerse Changelog**
**October 30, 2026**

🌏 **Gen 4 is here: Sinnoh's 107 Pokémon**
They don't appear in the wild yet, but your Pokémon can now evolve into their Sinnoh forms:
- **By level:** Ambipom (Aipom 40), Lickilicky (Lickitung 40), Tangrowth (Tangela 40), Yanmega (Yanma 40) and Mamoswine (Piloswine 60).
- **With a stone:** Magnezone (Magneton 60) and Probopass (Nosepass 40) with a Thunderstone, and Leafeon (Eevee 30) with a Leaf Stone.
- **12 new evolution items:**
  - Shiny Stone: Roserade, Togekiss
  - Dusk Stone: Mismagius, Honchkrow
  - Dawn Stone: Gallade, Froslass
  - Ice Stone: Glaceon
  - Razor Claw: Weavile (at night)
  - Razor Fang: Gliscor (at night)
  - Electirizer, Magmarizer, Protector, Reaper Cloth and Dubious Disc: Electivire, Magmortar, Rhyperior, Dusknoir and Porygon-Z
- Chansey drops the new Oval Stone. Stone buyers and the Poke Trader take all the new items.
- An item evolution now wins over a level one: a Kirlia holding a Dawn Stone becomes Gallade.
- The Pokepedia has a new **Evolution items** page.

💎 **Six new Mega Evolutions**
Mega Gardevoir, Mega Salamence, Mega Metagross, Mega Banette, Mega Mewtwo Y and Mega Latios. Their stones already exist, so they work right away.
- Mega Salamence brings **Aerilate**: Normal moves become Flying and hit 20% harder.
- Mega Banette brings **Prankster**: status moves recharge 30% faster.
- New art for Mega Sableye, Mega Absol, Mega Alakazam and Mega Latias.
```

## Post 2 of 3

```text
⚔️ **New moves**
- 24 legendary and signature moves, each with its own icon. Learn them now: Rayquaza (Dragon Ascent, 75), Rhydon (Rock Wrecker, 70), Arcanine (Raging Fury, 70), Ursaring (Headlong Rush, 75), Kingler (Liquidation, 75), Steelix (Body Press, 75) and Ninetales (Mystical Fire, 75).
- Feint is back: it breaks through Protect.
- 11 egg moves were misspelled and couldn't be learned. They work now.

🎨 **A fresh coat of paint**
- More than 80 Pokémon have new, more detailed sprites: Alakazam, Ponyta, Whiscash, Lickitung, Kangaskhan, Steelix, Ursaring, Ho-Oh, Aggron, Nidorino, Vulpix, Diglett and many more.
- 71 shinies were redone to match their new art and the real shiny colours.
- Noctowl, Gligar and Grovyle have new art too.

🏄 **Surf Pikachu and Raichu**
- New **Surfboard** addon in the Store (15 Verse Orbs): Pikachu and Raichu, shiny too, get a surf edition look and **can Surf** while they wear it.

🐢 **TMNT Blastoise**
- The Sword, Sai, Nunchaku and Staff Ninja Packs now fit Shiny Blastoise too, with green-skinned turtles.

🎖️ **Vocations**
- Press **Rank up** in the vocation window (U) once you've earned it: fireworks, and a banner with your new title and perks.
```

## Post 3 of 3

```text
📱 **Phones**
- The top bar keeps Store, Settings and Exit, now bigger. The rest folds into an **apps drawer**.
- Attack, Follow, Look and Use moved to a **"…" button** next to your moves.
- A smaller map ring, so Nearby gets more room. The chat sits beside the joystick, and the Pokémon bar starts top-left.
- The map ring's buttons show their icons again.
- The Day Care window fits small screens, so you can leave plates again.
- The Pokédex button reads the Pokémon you tap instead of opening your own dex.

🧭 **Interface**
- **Tab** targets in Nearby's order, with its sort and filters, starting from the top row.
- New top bar icons: a Pokédex for the Pokepedia and a backpack for the Bag.
- Analyser: the Catch tab explains each number, and a **?** button explains how catching works.
- Gen 2 and Gen 3 shinies can now be called by name from the quick-call bar.

🔧 **Fixes**
- The Bag opens while you're asleep, and the belt and bag tabs no longer go missing.
- The chat cursor stays put after a space.
- Day Care: the Old Lady opens the Day Care window when you say "daycare". Say "trade" for her incubators.

The update downloads on its own the next time you start the game.
```
