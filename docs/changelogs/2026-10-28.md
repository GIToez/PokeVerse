## Post 1 of 4

```text
📋 **PokeVerse Changelog**
**October 28, 2026**

📱 **Meet the Pokégear: a brand new look**
The whole screen has been rebuilt, and it's the new default.
- **Map ring** in the top-right: a round minimap with zoom and floor buttons, plus quick buttons for Friends, your Trainer Card, Quests, the Pokepedia and the Bag.
- **Nearby** replaces the Battle List: portraits, levels, HP bars and type dots. Filter Wild, Trainers, NPCs and Party, and sort by Distance, HP, Name or Level, up or down.
- **Bag (B):** your backpack as a grid, a tab for every bag, and your belt on top. Rename bags, and give them sort rules so loot goes straight where it belongs.
- **Belt switches:** Defense mode sends your Pokémon out when you're attacked, and Auto swap sends the next one out when yours faints.
- **Chat card:** channel bubbles, faces next to every line, and Enter to chat. WASD always walks. Fold it, drag it, stretch it from the corner, and lock it in place.
- **Tracking card** for your route, tasks, quests and tracked Pokémon. It folds into a chip under the map.
- **Social window:** Friends, Guild, Party and Blocked in one place.
- **Loot list** under your HUD, and a body window with Take all.
- **By your HUD:** your equipment slot (F), the Pokédex (X) and the badge case. Hover the XP bar for your XP rate and time to the next level.
- New-look right-click menus and tooltips.
- Drag any window anywhere. Settings → Interface → **Reset layout** puts everything back.
- Prefer the old screen? Settings → Interface → Layout: **Classic**.
```

## Post 2 of 4

```text
💎 **Mega Evolution is here!**
- From level 60, talk to Professor Oak in Pallet Town and say "mega".
- Defeat 50 wild Pokémon from each of three Mega Stone families, and Oak gives you an empty Mega Ring.
- At the very bottom of Cerulean Cave a crystal glows. Use it and defeat the Guardian Alakazam for the Key Stone, then put it on your ring.
- Show Oak your Mega Ring and he gives you the Mega Stone for your starter: Venusaurite, Blastoisinite, or Charizardite X or Y.

⚡ **How it works**
- Wear the Mega Ring in your key item slot and give your Pokémon its Mega Stone to hold.
- Fight to fill the new Mega gauge under your trainer HUD.
- At 100, press R or the Mega button: your Pokémon Mega Evolves for 60 seconds, with new stats, typing and ability.
- Moves its new ability boosts glow green on your move bar.
- It ends if you call your Pokémon back or it faints. No Mega Evolution in protection zones, and duels start everyone at 0.

🌟 **27 Mega forms and 2 Primals**
- The Kanto starters, Beedrill, Pidgeot, Alakazam, Slowbro, Gengar, Kangaskhan, Gyarados, Aerodactyl, Ampharos, Steelix, Scizor, Tyranitar, Sceptile, Blaziken, Swampert, Sableye, Mawile, Sharpedo, Camerupt, Altaria, Absol, Glalie and Latias.
- Shiny Pokémon become Shiny Megas.
- Groudon and Kyogre holding the Red or Blue Orb undergo Primal Reversion.
- Mega Stones drop very rarely from wild Pokémon of their own family, and the orbs from wild Groudon and Kyogre.
- The Pokédex shows each family's Mega: its stone, typing, ability and stats.
```

## Post 3 of 4

```text
✨ **Warp Styles**
- No more puff of smoke. Logging in, logging out and teleporting now play **Warp Spin**: a warp pad lights up, you spin faster and faster and zip into the sky, then land spinning where you arrive.
- Win a duel or trainer battle and you hop for joy in a gold burst with confetti. Lose one and you slump under a little rain cloud with dizzy stars.
- When your trainer faints, they float away as a glowing orb.
- ⭐ Premium trainers get **Beam**: a column of gold light scans you away and builds you back on arrival.
- Pick your styles in Customise Trainer → **Warp Styles**. Options → Interface → Warp styles: Everyone, Only mine, or Off.

🔮 **Soul Coins are now Verse Orbs**
- Same currency, new name and a new look: a purple glass orb with a galaxy turning inside. Your balance and the coins in your bag carry over as they are.
- **Buy Verse Orbs** in the Store with PayPal, Pay Later or a card. Your account needs a verified email.

🛒 **New in the Store**
- **Warp Styles:** watch each one on your own trainer before you buy. Poke Ball (10 Verse Orbs), plus Fireworks and Trophy victories, and Sweat Drop and Ghost defeats (5 each).
- **Pokémon costumes** in Trainer Outfits (15 Verse Orbs): Azurill or Blastoise, and Pikachu or Charizard.
- 🎃 **Halloween addons** (Featured): Frankenstein Cap (Psyduck), Clown Mask (Dewgong), Hannya Mask (Steelix) and Skull Cap (Sandshrew).
- **66 rare Pokémon addons** you couldn't get anywhere else, like Fossilized Kabuto and Omanyte, the Lapras pendants, the Feraligatr and Typhlosion costumes, and Pony and Horse Saddles.
- New **Customise Trainer** and **Pokémon Outfit** windows.
```

## Post 4 of 4

```text
🗺️ **Johto**
- **Tohjo Falls rebuilt:** twin waterfalls, a ledge path behind them, and a hidden staircase into a new lake cave with 94 wild Pokémon spawns.
- Violet, Goldenrod, Ecruteak, Olivine, Blackthorn and Cianwood get big Pokémon Centers with a trade center.
- Fixed half roofs and floating walls on Johto buildings. Marts are clear of roofs, Cherrygrove's pier reaches the water, and roads no longer run into cliffs.

📜 **Quests**
- Quest NPCs tell you when they have a task for you, and only once you're the right level for it. Quest levels are spread out more evenly.
- Quest log: **View on map** shows where to go with a bouncing arrow (the Pokepedia map uses it too). The older quests have names, and holiday quests sit under Events.
- The Running Shoes questline starts at level 30, and its Angry Beedrill is level 28 to 35.
- The old Tutorial Island quests are gone from the log.

🧭 **Interface**
- F1 to F6 stay with the same Pokémon. Right-click a slot → Move up / Move down to rearrange them.
- The red target ring no longer vanishes during fights.
- Trainer Card: your favorite Pokémon sticks through level-ups, plus a Skills box and your NPC battle record.
- Radar overlay: drag it, lock it, and tap the middle to scan again.
- Tutorial popups fit smaller screens and can be closed.
- Pokémon cry in the world with their X/Y voices.

🔧 **Fixes**
- Wearing the Mega Ring no longer empties your inventory at login.
- The pirate ships no longer fill up with copies of the Halloween NPCs.
- Fixed quick call not bringing your Pokémon out, the Analyser's Catch tab, Settings not opening, and updates that stopped partway on Windows.
- Money and event tokens can't go on the GTS or into the PC.
- Discord only announces shiny and legendary catches.

The update downloads on its own the next time you start the game.
```
