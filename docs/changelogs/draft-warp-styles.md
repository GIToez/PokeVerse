📋 **PokeVerse Changelog**

✨ **Warp Styles**
- Trainers no longer vanish in a puff of Tibia smoke. Logging in, logging out and teleporting now play **Warp Spin**: a warp pad lights up, you spin faster and faster and zip into the sky, and you land spinning where you arrive.
- Win a duel or a trainer battle and you hop for joy in a gold burst with confetti. Lose one and you slump grey under a little rain cloud with dizzy stars.
- When your trainer faints, they flash white and float away as a glowing orb.
- Everyone around you sees your styles.

⭐ **Premium: Beam**
- Premium trainers can use **Beam**: a column of gold light scans you away from the feet up, and builds you back on arrival.

🎨 **Pick your styles**
- Customise Trainer › **Warp Styles**: choose a style for Log in, Log out, Teleport, Victory, Defeat and Faint, and watch it on your trainer.

🛒 **New in the Store**
- **Warp Styles** category: watch each one play on your own trainer before you buy.
  - **Poke Ball** (10 Soul Coins): a Poke Ball's red light pulls you in, three wobbles and a click. Works for log in, log out and teleport.
  - **Fireworks** and **Trophy** victories, **Sweat Drop** and **Ghost** defeats (5 Soul Coins each).
- **Pokemon costumes** in Trainer Outfits (15 Soul Coins each): Azurill or Blastoise, and Pikachu or Charizard (the costume depends on your trainer's sex).
- More styles are coming.

⚙️ Options › Interface › **Warp styles**: Everyone, Only mine (everyone else shows the defaults), or Off.
