# October 31, 2026

Covers `main` after the October 30 changelog (`e18c32e7`), up to the merge of the PokeTrader auction
(#391). The art imports and the website trailer are left out, and so is the Surfboard addon, which the
October 30 changelog announced and which is replaced here by the Surfing Pikachu and Raichu species.
Three posts.

## Post 1 of 3

```text
📋 **PokeVerse Changelog**
**October 31, 2026**

🏝️ **Gen 4 in the wild: the Orange Islands**
About one in five spawns of each pre-evolution in the Orange Islands is now its Sinnoh evolution: Magnezone, Electivire, Magmortar, Rhyperior, Mamoswine, Togekiss, Tangrowth, Yanmega, Gliscor, Weavile, Honchkrow, Mismagius, Ambipom and Lickilicky.
- Gallade at the Forgotten Temple, Froslass and Glaceon on Ice Island, Dusknoir in Castle Fear, Probopass in Crystal Cave, Roserade in Pummelo Forest and Leafeon on Seven Grapefruit.
- **Porygon-Z** waits on the deepest floor of Hamlin's Power Plant.

🍼 **Baby Pokémon**
- The Orange Islands are a high-level area, so their wild babies grew up: Pichu became Raichu, Cleffa Clefable, Togepi Togetic and so on.
- Wild Togepi moved from Hoenn to **Route 30** in Johto. The 3 Wynaut in Hoenn are the only other wild babies.
- Babies are **passive**: they only fight back.
- But attack one and it **cries for its family**: every wild Pokémon of its line nearby, even from just off screen, comes for your Pokémon. Hit a Pichu and the Pikachu and Raichu around come running.
- **Eggs:** every Pokémon in a baby's line lays the baby's egg. Snorlax lays Munchlax, Mantine Mantyke, Chansey and Blissey Happiny, Mr. Mime Mime Jr., Sudowoodo Bonsly, Roselia Budew and Chimecho Chingling.
- Hitmonlee, Hitmonchan, Mr. Mime, Chansey and Blissey couldn't breed at all. Now they can, and the Hitmons lay Tyrogue eggs.
- **Breeding Weekend:** the weekend event now makes incubator eggs hatch twice as fast (30 minutes instead of 60), on top of the Breeder perk.
```

## Post 2 of 3

```text
💰 **The PokeTrader auction**
Tiger Kelsey now asks "Are you here for the auction?" Say **auction** to open the new auction window.
- Set your **maximum**: the most you'd pay. The auction bids for you, 1% over the next trainer, and never past your maximum. Nobody sees your maximum, only the price.
- Your whole maximum is held from your bank when you set it. Raise it any time; lower it or take it back while someone else leads.
- Bidding ends every **Sunday at 20:00** server time, with no extra time for late bids, and the next lots come out at once.
- The winner gets the item in their depot and the rest of their maximum back. Everyone else gets all of theirs back.
- If someone beats your maximum, you get a message.
- **My bids** shows your bids, the money held and what you won. **History** shows the last two weeks' winners.

🏄 **Surfing Pikachu and Surfing Raichu**
They replace the Surfboard addon: they're now their own rare Pokémon, shinies too.
- They appear on beaches in **summer**, and only while the sun is out: Slateport, Route 115, Lilycove, Tarroco, Tangelo, Great Beach and Fuchsia Shore.
- Sometimes a **beach swarm** of ten lands on one sunny beach for 30 minutes. The whole server is told.
- They learn **Surf** at level 35 and can travel on water.
```

## Post 3 of 3

```text
⚔️ **Moves**
- **13 new moves:** Lunar Dance (Cresselia), Ice Hammer (Abomasnow), Sparkling Aria (Lapras), Psyshield Bash (Stantler), Barb Barrage (Qwilfish), Raging Bull (Tauros), Rage Fist (Primeape), Hyper Drill (Dunsparce) and Dragon Hammer (Tropius). Belly Drum, Morning Sun, Whirlwind and Spikes now work as egg moves.
- Dragon Pulse was firing twice as often as it should. Its cooldown is fixed.
- Metal Burst, Shell Smash, Spider Web and Stealth Rock shared a cooldown with another move. Now they each have their own.
- Bug Bite's cooldown goes from 5 to 9 seconds, like Bite.
- **Pressure** now makes cooldowns 1.5x longer, not 2x, as its description says. Legendary fights drag less.

🔊 **Move sounds**
- Moves play their own sound, and it fades when the move ends.
- Options > Sound has new **Move Sounds** and **Pokemon Cries** sliders.

📈 **Skills**
- **Catching** no longer stops at 100. Each level makes the catch roll 0.1% smaller, up to 25.5% at 255.
- **Fishing and Headbutt** past 80: each level gives 2% more shiny chance and 1% chance of an extra Pokémon (shinies x1.4 and 20% at 100).

🎖️ **Vocations**
- **Medic:** a potion counts only on a hurt Pokémon without one running.
- **Breeder:** 1/2/3/4 eggs per rank instead of 2/4/6/10.
- **Catcher:** one shiny catch, for rank 5 only.

The update downloads on its own the next time you start the game.
```
